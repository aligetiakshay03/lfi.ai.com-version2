Linkfields is headquartered in Midrand, Johannesburg, South Africa, and has offices in India, the United States, the United Arab Emirates, Australia and Botswana. Its offshore delivery centre is in Hyderabad, India, and a United Kingdom presence is launching.

**How big is Linkfields and how long has it been operating?**

**What software products does Linkfields own?**
